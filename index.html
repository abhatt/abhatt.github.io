Arnab Bhattacharyya

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Warwick.

My research lies at the intersection of the foundations of artificial intelligence and theoretical computer science. I develop algorithms for learning from data, with particular interests in high-dimensional statistics, causal inference and discovery, and algorithms for big data.

I am part of the Foundations of AI & ML research group. I am also a co-director of the ELLIS Oxford Unit.
